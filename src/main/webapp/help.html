Staff help

Quick guidance for the main clinic workflows.

Register an appointment

Open Register appointment, enter the patient details, choose an active dentist and treatment, then select the date and time.

Search and review

Open Appointments and search with the complete appointment number, or select a row to view its details.

Edit or cancel

Open an active appointment to change its details. Cancellation keeps the record and releases the dentist time slot.

Manage patients

Open Patients to find a patient by name or contact number and update their contact details.

Billing

Open the bill from appointment details. The system stores one bill per appointment and provides a printable receipt.

Reports

Open Reports to review the daily summary, dentist activity, treatments, and revenue.
