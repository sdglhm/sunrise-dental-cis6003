Staff help

  1. Log in with your staff account.
  2. Register a patient appointment using their contact details.
  3. Choose the dentist, treatment, date and time.
  4. Use Find appointment and enter its appointment number to see the full record.
  5. Select Edit appointment to load its values, then save the changes or cancel the edit.
  6. Select Cancel appointment and confirm when an active appointment should no longer proceed.
  7. Use the date and dentist filters above the list to narrow the visible appointments.
  8. Select Preview bill for an active appointment to see the treatment fee, consultation fee and total.
  9. Use Logout when you finish.
