Sunrise Dental Clinic

Staff help

Quick guidance for the main clinic workflows.

Sign in and out

Use your staff username and password. Always log out from the sidebar when leaving the workstation.

Register an appointment

Enter the patient details, choose an active dentist and treatment, then select the appointment date and time.

Search and review

Search with the complete appointment number, or select an appointment from the table to open its details.

Edit or cancel

Open an active appointment to change its details. Cancellation keeps the record and releases the dentist time slot.

Billing and printing

Open the bill from appointment details. The system saves one bill per appointment and the receipt page can be printed from the browser.

Reports

Use the dashboard for today's summary. The reports section shows daily workload, dentist activity, treatments, and revenue.
